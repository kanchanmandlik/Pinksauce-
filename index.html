🍝 Pink Sauce Pasta Recipe

Creamy, Rich & Delicious Italian-Style Pasta

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 20 mins Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 250g Penne Pasta
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 4 Garlic Cloves (minced)
  • 1 medium Onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 cup Tomato Puree
  • 1/2 cup Fresh Cream
  • 1/4 cup Milk
  • 1 tsp Chili Flakes
  • 1 tsp Mixed Herbs
  • Salt to taste
  • Black Pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup Grated Parmesan Cheese
  • Fresh Basil for garnish

Instructions

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  3. Add garlic and onions; sauté until soft and fragrant.
  4. Add tomato puree and cook for 5-7 minutes until slightly thickened.
  5. Pour in fresh cream and milk, stirring continuously.
  6. Add chili flakes, mixed herbs, salt, and black pepper.
  7. Mix well and simmer for 2-3 minutes.
  8. Add the cooked pasta and toss until fully coated in the pink sauce.
  9. Stir in grated Parmesan cheese and cook for another minute.
  10. Garnish with fresh basil and serve hot.

Chef's Tips

  • Add sautéed mushrooms or grilled chicken for extra flavor.
  • Use heavy cream for a richer sauce.
  • Reserve some pasta water to adjust sauce consistency.
  • Serve with garlic bread and a fresh salad.
